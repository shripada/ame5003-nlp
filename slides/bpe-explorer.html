Merges are picked by highest weighted pair frequency. Ties are broken by which pair was first seen while scanning the corpus — the same rule classroom/demos/s03_bpe.py uses, which is why the default example above reproduces the exact six merges worked through on the slides. A merge stops being offered once every remaining pair occurs only once — merging it wouldn't compress anything.
